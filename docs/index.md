# About

Unit Circle was incorporated in 2014 and offers design services for embedded systems.  Typical projects include:

* Hardware design including part selection, schematic capture, PCB layout, and working with Contract Manufacturers to get assembled PCBs.
* Bare metal firmware development.   This can be for any of the embedded OSs, including Zephyr, Thread, FreeRTOS, etc.
* Driver development for sensors.
* Development of signal procesing and AI based algorithms for deployment on the embedded platform.
* Security solutions for device identity, authenticaion, in-field firmware updates, and communication with phone or backend services.
* Wireless communication over Bluetooth LE, LoRaWAN, and Cellular.
* Phone applications for iOS and/or Android to communicate with the embedded systems and backend services.
* Backend services that support data collection, traditional and AI based analysis, and monitoring push notifications for alerts.
